# Weather/outage remedies

## Question

Which operational remedy is most effective under the assumed weather/outage calendar: additional
crew/machine capacity, a smaller first-year scope, a longer window, or a combination?

The comparison uses all 20 rolling episodes, 50 calendars per episode, a 75-day base window, five
fixed outage days, and independent 20% daily weather loss. Added capacity is costed at 1,200 CAD per
crew-day and 900 CAD per machine-day against a 60,000 CAD first-year budget.

## Results

| Remedy | Scenario full completion | Added cost P50 | Total cost P50 | Budget feasible |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Baseline | 13.2% | 0 | 59,875 | 100% |
| +1 shared crew | 13.2% | 10,800 | 70,761 | 0% |
| +1 shared crew + machine | 92.9% | 80,850 | 140,800 | 0% |
| +1 shared machine | 13.2% | 8,100 | 68,061 | 0% |
| 10% scope reduction | 23.4% | 0 | 53,888 | 100% |
| 20% scope reduction | 44.9% | 0 | 47,900 | 100% |
| 10% scope + 90-day | 54.9% | 0 | 53,888 | 100% |
| 20% scope + 90-day | 66.4% | 0 | 47,900 | 100% |
| 90-day window | 41.5% | 0 | 59,875 | 100% |
| 105-day window | 61.9% | 0 | 59,875 | 100% |
| 20% scope + 105-day | **93.9%** | 0 | **47,900** | **100%** |

The 20% scope + 105-day combination is the best budget-feasible completion case.

## Interpretation

- A crew alone or machine alone does not improve completion because mowing and mechanical work
  require both. The pair is the strongest full-scope completion remedy.
- The crew-plus-machine case is not budget-feasible at prototype rates: median total first-year cost
  is about 140,800 CAD against 60,000 CAD.
- The 20% scope + 105-day case matches the completion of the capacity remedy at a lower budget cost,
  but it defers 20% of the first-year work and the risk effect is not evaluated.
- The 105-day window alone reaches 61.9% completion without added cost.
- 20% scope alone reaches 44.9%.

## Limits

- Added capacity and scope reduction are hypothetical tests, not contractor commitments.
- Crew-day and machine-day rates are prototype assumptions, not contractor quotes.
- Scope reduction applies proportionally to work-item hours; it is not a risk-ranked deferral policy,
  and deferred risk is not evaluated.
- Weather and outage assumptions are prototype values, not forecasts or asset-owner calendars.
- Only the first-year shift schedule is tested.
