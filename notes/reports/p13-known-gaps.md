# Known gaps at the 0.1.0 release candidate

1. **Field calibration.** The low-vegetation model is a prototype with synthetic calibration and
   validation. No BC/PNW field plot series or multi-cycle treatment records were read.
2. **Mature-forest transfer.** No mature-forest EFI card was transferred or refused with a measured
   test on the low-vegetation target.
3. **Live sibling integration.** FEMIC LiDAR/FreshForge, FHOPS, and fresh-road were represented by
   contracts, pins, dry runs, and offline adapters. No live production run was performed.
4. **Scale.** The strategic/tactical scale target is not demonstrated. P12 reports the measured small
   case and names the unatempted 10,000-segment/30-year/1,000-evaluation/8-core target.
5. **Public boundary.** The demo is public/synthetic and static. The lab preview is optional and
   read-only; no uploads, accounts, or private payloads are part of the public portal.
6. **Operational safety.** No claim of engineering clearance, dispatch readiness, contractor
   productivity certification, or safety release.
7. **3D fidelity.** The studio scene is a context view. It is not survey-grade, uses unknown-position
   labels, and does not invent tree or machine positions.
8. **Model uncertainty.** Monitoring VOI and planning outputs carry stated synthetic assumptions. They
   are not field-calibrated decision guarantees.
