Operations and maintenance
==========================

P14 records the monthly review cadence, dependency/pin audit, model/data governance register, maintainer handover guide, and synthetic pilot evidence. The machine-readable register is `notes/reference/p14-operations.v1.json`.

See `notes/operations/review-cadence.md`, `notes/operations/governance-register.md`, `notes/operations/maintainer-handover.md`, and `notes/reports/p14-pilot-evidence.md`.
